## Scientific consequence

Launching a founder tournament now would use population/evolutionary terminology while lacking
a valid payoff function. That is not evolutionary search; it is optimization against instrument
noise and shortcuts. Hall-of-Fame and surrogate infrastructure should stay dormant until at
least two independent, shortcut-clean mechanisms qualify under a protocol whose invalidation
rules are actually satisfied.

## Recommended sequence

1. Freeze/report Phase 6 as invalid, retaining the Qwen effect as descriptive evidence.
2. Repair Route-B/world task construction or build two real-state micro-environments.
3. Calibrate zero-model shortcuts and solver positive controls.
4. Only then compare founders under Game R.
5. Measure neighbor correlations before choosing local mutation; if absent, use diversity and
   architectural jumps.
6. Populate Hall of Fame only with results from the same frozen canonical/projection/task and
   verifier constitution, or label cross-constitution entries separately.
